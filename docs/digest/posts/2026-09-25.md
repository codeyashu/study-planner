## Question of the day

??? question "L3 — Your team wants a multi-agent system for customer-support triage. What do you ask before agreeing?"
    ??? success "Answer"
        Start from the task, not the architecture:

        - Can a single LLM call plus retrieval, or a fixed **workflow** (prompt chaining or routing), solve it? Anthropic's guidance is to add agentic autonomy only when a predefined path can't handle the variety of inputs.
        - Ask for the eval set first: 50–100 real tickets with expected outcomes.
        - Ask about the cost and latency budget per ticket.
        - Ask which tools need write access. Irreversible actions need human approval, and you need to check for the lethal trifecta.
        - Ask how failures are observed (tracing).

        Multi-agent adds coordination overhead and new failure modes, such as agents looping or losing context at hand-offs. Justify it with measured gains over a single agent on the eval set.

## English word of the day

**Trade-off (noun; also *to trade off*)** · *Register: neutral, standard in engineering.* · **Meaning:** a situation where you accept less of one good thing to get more of another. · **Example:** "The main trade-off is consistency versus latency." · **Style tip:** the noun is usually hyphenated (*trade-off*); the phrasal verb is two words (*we traded off latency for consistency*). Prefer *trade-off between A and B* to *trade-off of A and B*.
