# Podcasts & YouTube

Passive-learning channel: walks, commutes, chores. **Budget: max 3 podcasts + 4 channels.** Everything else is "search on demand".
Episode picks are marked *best entry point*; only pick ones you can find by title on the channel (links go to the show/channel, not guessed episode URLs).

## AI engineering podcasts

| Show | Focus | Why | Best entry point |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/podcast) | AI engineering, frameworks, labs | The one podcast for AI engineers; guests are framework authors and lab researchers. | Search the archive for episodes with the authors of the tools on your [radar](../trends/radar.md) (LangGraph, DSPy, Pydantic AI, MCP). |
| [Practical AI](https://practicalai.fm/) | Applied ML/LLMs | Approachable, production-flavoured interviews. | Episodes on evals and agent deployment. |
| [The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/) | Frontier AI, agents, safety | Long interviews with builders and researchers; good for the "where is this going" view. | Agent-infra and eval-related guests. |
| [Vanishing Gradients](https://vanishinggradients.fireside.fm/) :gem: | Hugo Bowne-Anderson interviews practitioners | Practitioner-heavy: evals, agents in production, the unglamorous parts. | Episodes with Hamel Husain and other eval/RAG practitioners. |
| [No Priors](https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast) | AI industry, founders, researchers | Strategy-level context useful for architect conversations. | Skim titles; listen selectively. |
| [Dwarkesh Podcast](https://www.dwarkesh.com/podcast) | Deep interviews with AI researchers | Long, prepared, technical — good for understanding scaling and reasoning debates. | Interviews with researchers on training/RL. |
| [TWIML AI Podcast](https://twimlai.com/podcast/) | ML research and industry | Long-running; strong on ML systems and applied research. | Systems/infra-focused episodes. |

## System design, architecture & engineering culture

| Show | Focus | Why | Best entry point |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Software Engineering Radio](https://se-radio.net/) | Deep technical interviews | Consistently senior-level: distributed systems, architecture, DDD. | Episodes with Kleppmann-, DDD- and architecture-era guests; use search. |
| [Software Engineering Daily](https://softwareengineeringdaily.com/) | Daily engineering interviews | Huge back catalogue; pick by topic. | Company-architecture episodes (search "architecture"). |
| [The Pragmatic Engineer Podcast](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/podcast) | Big-tech engineering, AI in dev | Career + org insight from Gergely's interviews. | Staff+/AI-engineering episodes. |
| [InfoQ Podcasts](https://www.infoq.com/podcasts/) | Architecture and QCon talks | Architects-and-trends podcast (Architects' Corner style). | Architecture/AI-platform episodes. |
| [The Changelog](https://changelog.com/podcast) | Open source, dev culture | Good OSS pulse; skim. | Episodes on Postgres, Python, and AI tooling. |
| [CoRecursive](https://corecursive.com/) :gem: | Story-driven software history | Narrative style makes hard engineering stories memorable. | Any episode on a famous system/bug. |
| [Oxide and Friends](https://oxide.computer/podcasts/oxide-and-friends) :gem: | Systems engineering, hardware/software | Deep systems people talking honestly about incidents and design. | Episodes on incident postmortems. |
